# FarmSense — 5 Demo Scenarios

Use these in Kibana Chat (or API) to test the full pipeline.

---

## 1. Maize, Nigeria — Drought + Fall Armyworm (CRITICAL)

**Input:**
> I'm growing maize in Oyo State, Nigeria. The leaves are turning yellow and curling at the edges. We've had very little rain for the last 3 weeks. The plants are at the 6-leaf stage.

**Expected:** RISK LEVEL HIGH or CRITICAL; primary diagnosis combining drought stress and Fall Armyworm; reference to outbreak in Osun (within 300 km); immediate actions (scout for FAW, conserve moisture).

---

## 2. Rice, Bangladesh — Flooding + Blast (HIGH)

**Input:**
> I'm growing rice in Dhaka, Bangladesh. After the recent floods, I'm seeing leaf spots and panicles turning brown. Very humid.

**Expected:** RISK LEVEL HIGH; rice blast mentioned; drainage and reduced N; possible fungicide mention.

---

## 3. Wheat, India — Normal Conditions, Minor Aphid (LOW)

**Input:**
> Wheat in Uttar Pradesh, India. I see some aphids on the leaves but the crop looks generally healthy. Normal rainfall this season.

**Expected:** RISK LEVEL LOW; aphid monitoring; natural enemies; avoid unnecessary insecticide.

---

## 4. Cassava, Kenya — Soil Nutrient Deficiency (MEDIUM)

**Input:**
> Cassava in Western Kenya. Leaves are yellowing and plants look stunted. Soil is poor and we haven't applied much fertilizer.

**Expected:** RISK LEVEL MEDIUM; nutrient (N/K) deficiency; compost/manure; small doses of fertilizer.

---

## 5. Tomato, Ethiopia — Late Blight + High Humidity (HIGH)

**Input:**
> Tomato in Oromia, Ethiopia. Leaves and stems have dark lesions; it's been cool and very humid. I'm worried about spread.

**Expected:** RISK LEVEL HIGH; late blight; improve airflow; copper or fungicide; resistant varieties.
