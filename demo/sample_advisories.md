## Example: Maize, Nigeria

**RISK LEVEL:** HIGH

**PRIMARY DIAGNOSIS:**  
Combination of drought stress and early Fall Armyworm (FAW) feeding.

**WHY THIS IS HAPPENING:**  
Rainfall in your area is about 43% below the 5-year average for the last 3 weeks, which causes maize leaves to roll and yellow. An active Fall Armyworm outbreak has been reported in Osun State (within 180 km), and FAW often causes similar leaf curling and window-pane feeding at the 6-leaf stage.

**IMMEDIATE ACTIONS (Next 48 Hours):**  
1. Scout the field for FAW larvae and damage; check for frass and feeding on whorls.  
2. If FAW is present, apply a recommended biopesticide (e.g. Bt or neem-based) following label rates.  
3. Conserve soil moisture with mulch or reduced tillage where possible.

**PREVENTIVE ACTIONS (Next 2 Weeks):**  
1. Plan for one or two strategic water applications at tasseling if the dry spell continues.  
2. Avoid late nitrogen application that can attract more FAW.  
3. Monitor for new FAW egg masses and larvae; repeat spray only if thresholds are met.

**WATCH FOR:**  
Rapid spread of FAW to neighbouring fields, or severe drought leading to crop failure—contact extension if more than 50% of plants are severely affected.
